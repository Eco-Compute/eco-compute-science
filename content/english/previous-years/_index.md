Before ecoCompute Science there was the **ecoCompute conference**, a large in-person
engineering conference on sustainability in hardware and software. It ran twice: Munich in
2024 and Berlin in 2025.

Organising an in-person event of that size is a lot of overhead, so there will be no
in-person ecoCompute in 2027. [ecoCompute Science](/) takes its place, online and
peer-reviewed.

Everything below stays online for reference. Slides from the 2025 talks are linked from the
2025 schedule.
