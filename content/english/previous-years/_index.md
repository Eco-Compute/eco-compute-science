ecoCompute Science is preceded by the **ecoCompute conference**, a large in-person
engineering conference on sustainability in hardware and software. It was held twice: in
Munich in 2024 and in Berlin in 2025.

Owing to the organisational overhead of an in-person event of that size, no in-person
ecoCompute will be held in 2027. [ecoCompute Science](/) takes its place, online and
peer-reviewed.

The material below remains available for reference. Slides from the 2025 talks are linked
from the 2025 schedule.
